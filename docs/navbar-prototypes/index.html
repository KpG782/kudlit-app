Kudlit Navbar — UX audit + 3 modern options

Tap the tabs in each phone below — every option here switches pages in one tap.

The problem today. FloatingTabNav is a 64 px pill in the bottom-right corner that shows only the current tab's icon. To switch you tap to expand, then tap a destination — 2 taps every time, and the other 3 destinations are invisible until you expand. That's the friction. All three options below fix it: destinations are always visible and switching is a single tap.
